    How to read this

    The card border

    Green means the genotype is generally favourable, red means generally unfavourable, grey means nobody has classified it or it is genuinely mixed. Traits and risk scores are always grey on purpose: a trait is not good or bad.

    Magnitude

    A 0 to 10 interest score computed on your machine from CPIC guideline level, ClinVar review stars, how many papers exist, how rare your genotype is, and whether you actually carry the variant. Every card can show the exact steps that produced its number. A blank magnitude means unscored and sorts as 1. This is not the SNPedia magnitude, which is hand written by wiki editors and cannot be redistributed here.

    Why some things say "not testable"

    A genoset is a rule over several positions. If your array did not genotype one of them the rule cannot be evaluated at all. That is different from having been checked and found absent, so it is reported separately.

    Flipped and ambiguous

    Testing companies report the plus strand; the reference databases sometimes store the other one. A flipped badge means the alleles were complemented to match, which is routine. An ambiguous badge means the genotype is A/T or C/G, where flipping gives the other observed allele, so the strand cannot be settled from the data at all. Treat those with caution and confirm with a clinical test before acting.
